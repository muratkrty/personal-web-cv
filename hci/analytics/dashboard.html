Top channels

    A channel is how a visit arrived: from a search engine (organic search), a social network such as the lab's YouTube videos, by typing the address (direct), or from a link on another site such as the school portal (referral).

    Acquisition by channel

    This table is how marketers measure a campaign: which channel brings visitors, whether they stay (bounce rate, pages per visit) and whether they sign up for a workshop (conversion rate).

    Clickstream: workshop sign-up funnel

    Clickstream analysis follows the sequence of pages each visitor clicks through. A funnel counts how many visits reach each step towards a goal, so you can see where people drop out.
